MothaSync

One drive alone is not gonna save you from data loss. Plug your working drive and your backup drive into the PC, and MothaSync mirrors them on its own — no hassle.

Download the app Install through 0install

Features

Tray app

Motha runs in the background and watches over your drives from the tray. Always there, never in the way.

Backed by rclone

Fast, reliable syncs powered by rclone — the tool trusted by millions.

Fire and forget

Set it up once and it just works. Motha syncs on login and whenever all drives connect.

Hot and cold

One drive travels, one stays home. MothaSync keeps the two in sync — your backup tool of choice picks the home drive up from there.

Reporting

Sync several PCs or set one up for someone else. You get a nudge as soon as drives go stale or a run fails.
